# Editorial image provenance

These images are navigation and article-header illustrations, not documentary photographs.
They must stay visibly labelled as AI-generated wherever they are used as a primary image.

## Source images

Generated on 2026-08-29 with `god-tibo-imagen` through the local Codex-authenticated provider. The request used a 16:9 editorial still-life direction; the returned files were 1672×941 pixels.

- `home-seoul-table.jpg`: a quiet Seoul morning table with Korean porcelain, banchan, stainless cutlery, muted rose bojagi and hanji; negative space was reserved for the homepage headline.
- `journal-field-notes.jpg`: a dark Korean field-note table with celadon, perilla leaves, a red pepper, stainless tableware, blank card, paper and a decorative knot.

Both prompts excluded people, faces, brands, logos, watermarks and readable text. A human visual review found none of those elements in the outputs. No stock photograph or family photograph was supplied as a reference.

## Derived crops

The following 1672×941 files are local crops of the two source images, made with macOS `sips`; they did not trigger another model call:

- `dining-table.jpg` and `family-table.jpg` derive from `home-seoul-table.jpg`.
- `culture-notes.jpg` and `transit-card.jpg` derive from `journal-field-notes.jpg`.

Each crop has a 700×394 `.thumb.jpg` sibling for listing cards. Full images are used for article headers and social metadata.

All final JPEGs were re-encoded through a browser canvas after cropping. The published files retain the documented dimensions but contain no EXIF, IPTC, XMP or Photoshop metadata blocks.

## Publishing rules

- Keep the visible “AI-generated editorial still life” note and the link to `/editorial-policy/`.
- Do not describe these images as a real family event, location, meal or purchase.
- Do not use them as evidence for a factual claim.
- Recheck the image-provider terms before redistribution outside this site.
