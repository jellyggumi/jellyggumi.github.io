## Platform policy cross-check

The retained principles were checked against published guidance, which is the
authoritative layer wherever the video and policy disagree:

- Google, people-first helpful content guidance:
  `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content`
- Google, spam policies including scaled content abuse:
  `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies`
- Google, current generative-AI content guidance (AI can help research and
  structure original content; scaled generation without user value can violate
  spam policy; accuracy, quality, relevance and creation context remain required):
  `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content`
- Naver Search Advisor's official content guidance (expertise or honest
  experience, a consistent site topic, source attribution, readable structure
  and freshness; when AI is used, do not stop at generation or summary and add
  the operator's experience and perspective):
  `https://searchadvisor.naver.com/guide/content-basic`
  The page does not itself require an AI-use disclosure; JellyGGumi keeps
  visible disclosure as its own stricter trust control.

Convergent conclusion: scaled, low-originality automation is explicitly unsafe;
a consistent owned topic, demonstrated expertise, original contribution and
useful next reader actions align with current guidance. JellyGGumi additionally
requires a transparent creation method as a stricter site-level trust control.

## Mapping to JellyGGumi automation

Scheduled automation cannot truthfully claim that a human wrote or reviewed
each nightly post, and this journal never fakes that. Instead of imitating the
video's exemplar blogger, the harness substitutes enforceable equivalents:

- Owned topic → five pinned content pillars and guide-only automation.
- Personal perspective → a mandatory original-contribution kind bound to
  verified evidence, validated per package.
- Reader's next action → one clean internal `/journal/<slug>/` related-guide
  link declared in the brief and present in the body.
- Human judgment → transparent policy-bound AI assistance, an exact visible
  AI-role disclosure sentence, independent evidence review and fail-closed
  validation under standing human-granted authority.
- Anti-scaling → one guide per run maximum, valid empty runs, and the
  `no-scaled-content-automation` standing constraint.
