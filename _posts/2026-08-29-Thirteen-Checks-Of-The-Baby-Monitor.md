<p><strong>Field note:</strong> adapted from a short Korean diary entry I wrote on 17 October 2024 about an afternoon on 12 October. The original ran to a few lines and a handful of photographs, and this is not much longer.</p>

<p>The air had finally turned. That is the point in the Korean autumn when everyone I know starts saying the word camping, and I would normally have agreed with all of them. Our daughter was not yet 150 days old. Pitching a tent and staying out overnight with her was not a plan, it was a fantasy with a night feed attached.</p>

<p>So the plan shrank into something that fit: a blanket by the Han River, in the afternoon, with people I like. And my husband keeping the baby at home, which is the entire reason the rest of this happened. Thank you, again, in writing.</p>

<p>My ambitions for the day, stated in advance and in full: let me drink something, let somebody feed my kid her milk, and then let me go home.</p>

<h3>Check one, in full sun</h3>

<p>We arrived while the light was still hard and bright, put everything down, and stayed in that one spot until night. At some point I opened the camera. She was fine.</p>

<!--post-ad-break-->

<h3>The middle checks, as the sun went down</h3>

<p>The afternoon slid sideways into evening. We talked, laughed and ate, exactly as I described it in the original note.</p>

<p>Somewhere in that stretch I opened the camera again. Then again. I missed her in the middle of the afternoon away that I had wanted.</p>

<p>I noticed I was not the only one. Every so often somebody else on the blanket would go quiet, look at their phone for a moment, and put it away again. Then the same person would say how good it was to be out.</p>

<h3>The last checks, in the dark</h3>

<p>By night the riverside was crowded, and somehow that made it quieter. All those people, all that noise, and inside it a small pocket of calm where I could hear myself. That was the part I had not expected and the part I kept.</p>

<p>I opened the camera about thirteen times in total. I wrote that down at the time as a confession, and I do not think it needs one. The missing ran alongside the afternoon rather than spoiling it.</p>

<p>Then I asked for another one, in writing, in the tone of someone who is joking and is not joking at all. Next time, please. Are you reading this?</p>
