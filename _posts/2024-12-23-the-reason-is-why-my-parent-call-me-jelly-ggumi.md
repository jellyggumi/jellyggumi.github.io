<h3>The Birth Story of JellyGGumi</h3>

<p>The name “Jelly GGumi” was given to me by my mom, inspired by my teddy bear-like appearance, soft and squishy like jelly. “GGumi” was actually my prenatal nickname, which means “dream” in Korean, and when pronounced cutely, it sounds like “GGumi.” My mom thought it sounded like a word that brings to mind a cuddly little bear, so she decided to call me GGumi. Isn’t the name itself already adorable?</p>

<p>The first time my mom saw me was when I was just a tiny little dot in her womb at five weeks. As the baby’s space in her belly grew, my tiny form grew too, and I started to look more and more like a jelly bear.</p>
