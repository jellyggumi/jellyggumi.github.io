This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit JellyGGumi Growth Diary (https://jellyggumi.github.io/), how it is used, and the choices available to you. This site is a personal blog, not a company.

Information This Site Collects

This site does not ask you to create an account and does not require you to submit personal information in order to read its content. No registration, login or subscription is needed.

Standard technical information is collected automatically when any website is visited. This can include your approximate location derived from your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, and which pages you viewed. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use its pages, for example which posts are read most and which countries readers come from. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data on our behalf. This data is aggregated and used to improve the site's content.

You can find more information about how Google uses data from sites that use its services in Google's own policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-Party Advertising and Cookies

This site uses third-party advertising, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of personalised advertising from other participating vendors at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Third-party advertising vendors may collect and use data according to their own privacy policies, which are not controlled by this site.

Managing Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Cookies are used here for analytics and advertising as described above.

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies entirely, delete existing cookies, or be warned before one is stored. Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of this or other websites function.

Comments and Contact

If a commenting system is enabled on a post, any information you choose to submit in a comment, such as a name or email address, is handled by that comment provider under its own privacy policy.

If you contact the site owner by email, your message and email address are used only to reply to you and are not shared with third parties or used for marketing.

External Links

This site links to external websites that it does not operate or control. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit through a link here.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example if new services are added to the site. Any changes will be published on this page along with a revised update date below.
