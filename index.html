Why this site exists

Useful context, not a tourism checklist

Everyday Korean context

The journal explains the unwritten part of a routine: when to tap a transport card, how a barbecue table divides the work, or why a Korean word on a sign matters more than its romanisation.

A real family record

The diary and albums document INa's milestones and family travel. The linked Korean-language Naver archive contains the longer day-to-day record behind this English site.

Transparent editing

AI may assist research and drafting, but it is not treated as a source. The editorial policy explains the workflow, correction standard and what readers should verify before relying on a changing detail.

Behind JellyGGumi

Written from a home in Korea

JellyGGumi is maintained by INa's parent, living in Korea. It is a personal publication rather than a travel agency or official authority. That boundary matters: lived observations are labelled as such, changing rules should be checked against the linked official source, and corrections are welcomed.
