{%- comment -%} The single adsbygoogle loader for the whole page, emitted from . Everything about whether ads exist on this site is decided here: - `site.google_ad_client` empty -> no AdSense on the site at all; - non-production build -> no AdSense, so local previews and `bundle exec jekyll serve` never call Google and never risk an invalid-traffic finding from the author's own reloads; - 404 / search pages -> no AdSense. Google's policy is that ads sit beside content the publisher provides. A 404 has none, and an empty search page has none until the reader types something. Neither is a destination anyone lands on from search, so serving ads there buys nothing and risks a policy finding. The individual ad units (`adsense-post.html`, `adsense-in-article.html`) repeat the production/client test rather than trusting this include, because a unit rendered without its loader is an empty that never fills. `google-adsense-account` is the ownership meta tag AdSense looks for while it reviews a site; it is what lets the crawler tie this domain to the publisher account even on a page where no ad unit happens to render. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ads_page = true -%} {%- if page.url == '/404.html' or page.url == '/search/' -%}{%- assign ads_page = false -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if jekyll.environment == 'production' and site.google_ad_client != empty and site.google_ad_client and ads_page -%} {%- endif -%}
