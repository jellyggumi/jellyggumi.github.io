{%- comment -%} Keep ownership verification separate from ad delivery. The ownership meta tag is safe on every production page and gives AdSense a stable way to associate the site with its publisher. The ad loader is much narrower: it appears only on full editorial posts (or a page that explicitly opts in with `adsense: true`). This prevents account-level Auto ads from being placed on the home page, taxonomy/archive listings, galleries, games, search, legal pages or other screens whose job is navigation rather than reading. A page marked noindex is never ad-eligible, even if its layout is `post`. Local previews remain entirely disconnected from AdSense. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ads_page = false -%} {%- if page.layout == 'post' or page.adsense == true -%} {%- assign ads_page = true -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if page.robots contains 'noindex' or page.ads == false -%} {%- assign ads_page = false -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if jekyll.environment == 'production' and site.google_ad_client != empty and site.google_ad_client -%} {%- if ads_page %} {%- endif %} {%- endif -%}
