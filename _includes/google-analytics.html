{%- comment -%} Google Analytics 4, emitted from . The measurement ID used to be hard-coded twice in head.html — once in the loader URL and once in the gtag('config') call — so changing property meant editing a template and remembering both occurrences. It now comes from `google_analytics.id` in _config.yml, and emptying that value takes analytics off the site entirely. Production-only, for the same reason as AdSense: a local `jekyll serve` should not push the author's own reloads into the property and skew the numbers of a site that measures single-digit daily users. Unlike ads, analytics is *not* withheld from the 404 and search pages. The reasoning is the opposite: a 404 has no content to place an ad beside, but knowing that readers are landing on one is exactly the signal worth having. The property currently records views on "Page not found", and those are the broken inbound links worth fixing. The snippet stays `async` with an immediate `gtag('js')`, i.e. Google's own published form, rather than being deferred behind DOMContentLoaded. Deferring it would drop the shortest sessions and quietly change what the property measures; this change is meant to move where the ID lives, not to move the numbers. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if jekyll.environment == 'production' and site.google_analytics.id != empty and site.google_analytics.id -%} {%- endif -%}
