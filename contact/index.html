Hello. This site is written and maintained by one person, so every message arrives straight in my inbox rather than a support queue. Email is the only channel; skipping a contact form means no extra service needs to collect your details before your note reaches me.

Email

Write to {{ site.email }}. Replies are usually sent within a few days. Messages in English or Korean are both fine.

What I am Happy to Hear About

  • Corrections. If something on this site is wrong, out of date, or misleading, this is the most useful message you can send. Please include the page and what specifically is wrong, and it will be checked and amended.
  • Questions about a post. If a guide left something unclear, say so — that gap is usually worth fixing in the post itself, so the answer helps everyone who reads it afterwards.
  • Photo permissions. Photographs here are family photographs and are not licensed for reuse. If you want to use one, ask first and explain where and how.
  • Suggestions. If you came looking for something about living in or visiting Korea and could not find it, please say so. That missing answer is often where the next useful guide begins.

What This Site Does Not Do

To set expectations honestly: this is a personal blog, not a business or a travel agency. It does not accept paid guest posts, sponsored placements, or link-insertion requests, and those emails will not receive a reply. It also cannot arrange bookings, act as a translator, or give individual visa, legal, medical or financial advice.

About the Author

Details about who writes this site and why are on the about page. How your data is handled when you visit, including cookies and advertising, is covered in the privacy policy.
