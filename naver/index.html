Most of the day-to-day writing behind this site lives on Naver Blog, Korea's largest blogging platform. Those posts are in Korean and cover parenting, travel with a baby, cafes and restaurants around Dongtan and Hwaseong, and product reviews. This page indexes them so they can be found from here.

Two blogs are listed. 그럭저럭 흘러가는 일상기록 is the older of the two and covers a wedding, IVF, pregnancy and the first months of parenthood, alongside local restaurant and cafe write-ups. 젤리처럼 달콤한 꾸미의 일상 is the newer one and follows Ggumi's growth, including travel abroad with an infant.

Dates below are the original publication dates in Korean time (KST). Link labels may be shortened to omit promotion codes. Original posts can contain historical promotions or product-provision disclosures; a link here is an archive reference, not a current offer or endorsement.
